# RFR-2026-015: Automated confidence-decay and frontier refresh

## Research opportunity

Make conclusions time-aware and regenerate frontier candidates when evidence, dependencies, or status change.

## Background

The repository has dashboards and registries but no consistent machine-readable confidence history or decay trigger.

**Specific assumption challenged:** Static confidence labels remain current as evidence and technology change.

**Supporting evidence:** Generated indexes can be stale and omit confidence/lineage query fields; current narratives already conflict.

- Trigger sensitivity
- How to avoid automating epistemic authority

## Suggested REP and methodology

Create `REP-2026-015`. Define claim-type-specific review intervals and event triggers; retain human approval; build check-only stale-state detection and an append-only confidence history.

- Confidence-history schema
- Decay policy experiment
- Frontier refresh checker

## Success criteria

Stale or contradicted conclusions are flagged reproducibly without automatically changing accepted claims.
