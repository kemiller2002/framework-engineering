# RFR-2026-009: Cross-provider reproducibility semantics

## Research opportunity

Define and test provider/model/role/run lineage so replication claims distinguish independence from repetition.

## Background

Provider-specific effects are material and ROS currently lacks machine-resolved reproducibility semantics.

## Origin documents and trace

**Specific assumption challenged:** Different provider labels alone imply independent replication.

**Supporting evidence:** The dashboard says provider differences remain material; the audit says dependencies and execution lineage are documentary.

## Unknowns

- Operational degrees of independence
- Minimum lineage needed for replication
- Provider-by-instrument interaction

## Dependencies

- [RFR-2026-008](./RFR-2026-008.md)

## Suggested REP and methodology

Create `REP-2026-009`. Specify an independence taxonomy and lock record; validate it on repeated same-model, cross-model, cross-provider, and human runs.

## Expected outputs

- Replication semantics standard
- Machine-readable lineage schema
- Validation cases

## Success criteria

Consumers can mechanically distinguish rerun, robustness check, and independent replication.
