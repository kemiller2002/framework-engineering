## Knowledge extraction

- **Primary objective:** Characterize 100 knowledge artifacts across identity, capability, lifecycle, and composition.
- **Primary claims:** The corpus covers diverse artifact identities and capabilities. Most current entries are established artifacts.
- **Methodology:** Batch-based single-analyst characterization under FEMS-1.
- **Accepted hypotheses/findings:** The corpus contains 100 indexed artifacts.
- **Rejected or unsupported hypotheses:** Current frequency distributions are validated population estimates.
- **Assumptions:** The sample is sufficiently representative. Labels are reliable despite single-analyst coding.
- **Limitations:** All characterizations are draft; capability assignments are provisional; composition is sparse.
- **Known uncertainties:** Inter-rater reliability; Sampling bias; Label stability; Primitive saturation.
- **Confidence:** bounded by the source package; no confidence is promoted by this frontier analysis.

## Adversarial challenge

The corpus is operationally treated as an evidence base while its statistics explicitly describe all characterizations as draft. Evidence that would materially change the assessment includes independent replication, preregistered negative controls, matched causal comparison, or a demonstrated failure of the current measurement instruments. The analysis deliberately treats planned validation as a dependency, not as completed evidence.
