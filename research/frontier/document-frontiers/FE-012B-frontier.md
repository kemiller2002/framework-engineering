## Knowledge extraction

- **Primary objective:** Test whether a frozen primitive vocabulary can synthesize coherent structures for novel problems.
- **Primary claims:** A small backbone appears useful. Coordination-heavy problems expose missing grammar. Generic coherence is not domain adequacy.
- **Methodology:** Novel problem synthesis followed by methodological review.
- **Accepted hypotheses/findings:** Bound, Observe, Evaluate, Decide, and Act appear indispensable within this run.
- **Rejected or unsupported hypotheses:** The current vocabulary is sufficient across execution-heavy domains.
- **Assumptions:** Novel problems do not trigger memorized framework reconstruction. Review criteria distinguish generic from domain-specific coherence.
- **Limitations:** Independent reviewers and larger novelty samples are absent.
- **Known uncertainties:** Coordination primitives; Memorization effects; Independent composition agreement.
- **Confidence:** bounded by the source package; no confidence is promoted by this frontier analysis.

## Adversarial challenge

The analysis deliberately treats planned validation as a dependency, not as completed evidence.
