# A01 — Identity-capability separation

- **Input [direct]:** A knowledge artifact for which single-label classification is unstable because artifacts overlap in function (theory doc, Observation 1).
- **Operation [direct]:** Separate what an artifact is (identity) from what it can do (capabilities) rather than assigning one classification label (theory doc, Observation 1; Theory v0.1 claim 1).
- **Output [inference]:** An artifact description in which identity and capability are recorded as distinct properties rather than merged into one label.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Knowledge-artifact characterization work, specifically the FE-008 context where artifacts "overlap too much for single-label classification to remain stable."
- **Failure conditions [absent]:** No canonical failure condition is stated in the permitted evidence. The theory doc states the problem the mechanism responds to, not a test for when the separation itself fails.
- **Predicted effect [direct]:** "Knowledge artifacts are easier to reason about when identity is separated from capabilities" (Theory v0.1 claim 1).
- **Measurement [absent]:** No metric (e.g., reviewer agreement) is defined in the permitted canonical set.

The identity-capability claim rests on a single internal evidence source (FE-008) referenced only by name, and its independent-reviewer stability is an open question rather than a settled result.
