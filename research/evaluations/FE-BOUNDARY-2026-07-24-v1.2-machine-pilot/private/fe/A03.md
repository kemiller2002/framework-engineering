# A03 — Finite primitives composed into procedural structure

- **Input [inference]:** A procedural artifact whose reasoning steps can be described independently of domain labels, consistent with the definition of "Primitive Reasoning Operation" as "a provisional low-level reasoning function used to describe procedural structure independently from domain labels" (theory doc, Core Definitions).
- **Operation [direct]:** Identify primitive reasoning operations and compose them into a "Procedural Reasoning Grammar," defined as "a hypothesized composition of reasoning primitives that structures analysis, diagnosis, evaluation, choice, verification, reflection, and reassessment" (theory doc, Core Definitions).
- **Output [inference]:** A primitive-level representation of an artifact's reasoning structure, sufficient to attempt reconstruction or synthesis of procedural structure (inferred from Observations 3 and 4's discussion of "reconstruct" and "synthesize").
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Reasoning-heavy procedural artifacts; the theory doc's fourth provisional claim states "primitive sufficiency appears weaker in coordination-heavy problems than in reasoning-heavy problems."
- **Failure conditions [direct]:** The falsification criteria state the theory weakens if "independent extractors do not converge," "primitive vocabulary keeps expanding," or "reconstruction fails outside familiar domains."
- **Predicted effect [direct]:** "Independent extractors will converge more strongly on reasoning-heavy artifacts than on coordination-heavy artifacts" and "primitive vocabulary growth will slow for reasoning artifacts before it slows for coordination artifacts" (Predictions).
- **Measurement [inference]:** Vocabulary growth and extractor convergence are implied as the measured quantities by the predictions above, though no scoring procedure is defined in the permitted set.
- **Implementation test [absent]:** The extraction, normalization, reconstruction, and holdout-testing procedure described in the v1.1 lead card is not present in this role's permitted canonical set and is therefore not asserted here.
- **Decision rights [absent]:** Not addressed.
- **Uncertainty handling [inference]:** Observation 3 notes vocabulary stabilization was not complete and reconstruction results were mixed ("strong or moderate... for most artifacts"), implying uncertainty about vocabulary status should be preserved rather than treated as settled; this is an inference from the hedged observation wording.
- **Verification [direct]:** Next Required Work calls to "Re-run FE-012A with independent extractors and audited merge disagreements" and "Re-run FE-012B with independent reviewers and a larger set of problems not previously used."
- **Provenance [absent]:** Not addressed in the permitted canonical set.
- **Implementation cost [absent]:** Not addressed.
- **Limitations [direct]:** Observation 3 states the internal run "showed [incomplete] vocabulary stabilization" and "remains vulnerable to same-model bias and extractor non-independence." Observation 4 states coordination-heavy problems "exposed missing primitive pressure" against the current frozen vocabulary. Open Questions ask whether `Compare` and `Prioritize` are durable primitives or temporary repair terms, and whether the theory can "survive independent human testing rather than internal same-model runs."
