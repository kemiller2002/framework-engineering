# Recognition pretest

For every card, select exactly one source category: method construction; systems lifecycle; requirements/traceability; workflow/process modeling; provenance/reproducibility; analytic/decision practice. Record confidence from 0 to 1 and any phrase that revealed the category. Do not search and do not discuss guesses.

Pass only when two or more new participants yield mean accuracy ≤0.40 and no A-series card accuracy >0.60. Failed cards must be revised and tested with new participants.
