# A08 — Integrated characterization-to-reassessment workflow

- **Input [inference]:** A knowledge artifact, its purpose and uses, evidence, observed weaknesses, and a prospective test.
- **Operation [direct/inference]:** Characterize the artifact; redesign it with evidence-linked structure; test the result; route observations through an evidence decision record and review before selecting the next experiment or reassessment.
- **Output [direct/inference]:** Characterization, redesigned artifact, test observations, evidence decision record, review outcome, and next action.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** The current research program is stronger as characterization and redesign work than as framework generation; findings remain provisional.
- **Failure conditions [direct/inference]:** Redesign does not improve outputs, added structure reduces usefulness, or a downstream experiment starts before accepted review selects it.
- **Predicted effect [absent]:** No non-additive interaction or sequencing effect is specified.
- **Measurement [absent]:** No bundle-level interaction contrast is defined.
- **Named baseline [absent]:** No matched workflow baseline is frozen.
- **Boundary condition [direct]:** Product use does not validate research; evidence must survive review before informing design.
- **Implementation test [inference]:** From a frozen input, independently produce every stage artifact and verify that the accepted review, not intuition, selects the next action.
- **Decision rights [direct]:** Evidence decision record precedes hypothesis review; accepted review output governs downstream selection.
- **Uncertainty handling [direct]:** Preserve negative, mixed, and uncertain findings; confidence is not updated directly from a run.
- **Verification [direct]:** Review after evidence decision record; redesign claims require stronger controlled follow-on tests.
- **Provenance [direct/inference]:** Carry source, redesign, test, evidence-decision, and review links through the flow.
- **Implementation cost [direct/inference]:** Multi-stage structure adds complexity and overhead.
- **Limitations [direct]:** No demonstrated bundle interaction; internal evidence; possible generic-structure explanation.
- **Fidelity check [inference]:** No stage silently changes confidence or bypasses review.
- **Null-capable future experiment [inference]:** Factorial or ablation study can find no interaction, no sequencing gain, or increased overhead.

## Statement basis

- **Direct:** Program scope, evidence-to-review flow, decision constraints, uncertainty rules, and known limitations.
- **Inference:** Bundle input/output, consolidated operation, test, provenance, fidelity check, and future study.
- **Proposal-only:** None beyond provisional theory and heuristics.
- **Absent:** Interaction model, bundle prediction, metric, and matched baseline.
