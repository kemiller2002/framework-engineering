# A01 — Identity-capability separation

- **Input [direct]:** A knowledge artifact and evidence about its dominant purpose and functions.
- **Operation [direct]:** Assign identity from dominant purpose; record functions separately as capabilities; do not conflate the two. Record composition only when reasoning structure exists.
- **Output [direct]:** Separate identity and capability descriptions; composition and domain may also be recorded.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Knowledge-artifact characterization, especially where capabilities overlap across artifact identities.
- **Failure conditions [direct]:** Capabilities become indistinguishable from identity, or reviewer disagreement does not improve.
- **Predicted effect [direct]:** Separation should improve classification consistency and reduce reviewer disagreement.
- **Measurement [direct]:** Reviewer agreement and ambiguity under the separated instrument.
- **Named baseline [inference]:** A single-label or undivided characterization instrument.
- **Boundary condition [inference]:** Dominant purpose and functions must be recoverable from the available artifact evidence.
- **Implementation test [inference]:** Independent reviewers characterize the same artifacts with separated and undivided instruments; the candidate fails if agreement and ambiguity do not improve.
- **Decision rights [absent]:** Canonical records do not assign who resolves disputed identity.
- **Uncertainty handling [inference]:** Preserve unresolved identity or capability assignments rather than forcing a label.
- **Verification [direct]:** Larger blind reviewer comparison is required.
- **Provenance [inference]:** Retain artifact evidence supporting each identity and capability entry.
- **Implementation cost [absent]:** Not estimated in current records.
- **Limitations [direct]:** Pilot-derived hypothesis; larger independent review and comparison with alternative schemes remain required.
- **Fidelity check [inference]:** Identity is based on dominant purpose, capabilities remain separately listed, and composition is not invented.
- **Null-capable future experiment [direct/inference]:** The prescribed independent comparison can return no agreement improvement or no ambiguity reduction.

## Statement basis

- **Direct:** Rules, hypothesis, predicted agreement effect, success/failure criteria, and need for a larger blind comparison.
- **Inference:** Baseline wording, boundary, implementation procedure, uncertainty treatment, fidelity check, and item-level provenance.
- **Proposal-only:** None.
- **Absent:** Dispute authority and implementation-cost estimate.
