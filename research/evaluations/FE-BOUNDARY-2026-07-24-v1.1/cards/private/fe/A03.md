# A03 — Finite primitives composed into procedural structure

- **Input [direct]:** Blinded procedural flow retaining reasoning steps, decision structure, and evaluation criteria while masking identity and domain cues.
- **Operation [direct]:** Extract reasoning verb phrases with context and confidence; cautiously normalize them into candidate primitives; document merges and disagreements; track vocabulary growth; reconstruct flow from primitive sequences. A separate proposal extracts entries, exits, conditions, branches, loops, invariants, termination, and procedural trees.
- **Output [direct]:** Extraction records, normalized vocabulary, growth history, primitive sequences, reconstructions, and unresolved merges; the newer proposal adds structural representations.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Reasoning-heavy procedural artifacts; current evidence is weaker for representational, classificatory, and coordination-heavy artifacts.
- **Failure conditions [direct]:** Domain drift, continuing vocabulary growth, poor reconstruction, or failure of independent extractors to converge.
- **Predicted effect [direct]:** Independent extractors should converge more on reasoning-heavy artifacts than coordination-heavy artifacts; vocabulary growth should slow sooner for reasoning artifacts.
- **Measurement [direct]:** Vocabulary growth, extractor agreement, reconstruction strength, ambiguity, and merge disagreements.
- **Named baseline [absent]:** No matched free-form encoding baseline is frozen.
- **Boundary condition [direct]:** Vocabulary must be frozen before holdout testing; internal product artifacts are excluded from initial extraction.
- **Implementation test [direct]:** Blind extraction, cautious normalization, reconstruction from sequences, then holdout transfer without vocabulary modification.
- **Decision rights [direct]:** Extractor proposes; disagreements and uncertain candidates are preserved rather than forced.
- **Uncertainty handling [direct]:** Record confidence, notes, disagreements, and rejected merges.
- **Verification [direct]:** Independent extractor comparison and merge-disagreement audit.
- **Provenance [direct]:** Raw verb phrase, surrounding context, inferred operation, confidence, and notes.
- **Implementation cost [inference]:** Blinding, extraction, normalization, reconstruction, reconciliation, and holdout passes.
- **Limitations [direct]:** Single extractor and same model family in the internal run; prior research awareness; subjective recognizability; normalization and selection bias.
- **Fidelity check [direct/inference]:** No artifact names or domain terms drive primitives; every merge is logged; the frozen vocabulary is unchanged during holdout.
- **Null-capable future experiment [direct]:** Independent extraction can show non-convergence, continued vocabulary growth, or failed reconstruction.
