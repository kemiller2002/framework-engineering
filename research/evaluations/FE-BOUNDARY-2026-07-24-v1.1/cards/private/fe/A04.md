# A04 — Separate reasoning and coordination grammars

- **Input [inference]:** A procedural artifact containing both reasoning functions and timing, allocation, synchronization, sequencing, or coordinated action.
- **Operation [proposal-only]:** Represent reasoning functions in one grammar and execution/coordination functions in another; retain possible bridge operations without assuming their status.
- **Output [proposal-only]:** Two related representations plus recorded bridge candidates and unresolved entanglements.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** The hypothesis is motivated where coordination-heavy synthesis pressures the current reasoning vocabulary.
- **Failure conditions [direct]:** The grammars cannot be defensibly separated, or coordination-heavy work succeeds after only minor refinement of one grammar family.
- **Predicted effect [direct]:** Coordination-heavy artifacts will request additional execution primitives more often than diagnostic artifacts; extractor convergence should be higher for reasoning-heavy artifacts.
- **Measurement [direct]:** Missing-primitive requests and independent extractor convergence by artifact family.
- **Named baseline [inference]:** One refined grammar used for both reasoning and coordination.
- **Boundary condition [direct]:** Candidate execution operations have not been independently extracted from blinded artifacts.
- **Implementation test [direct]:** Blind extraction and synthesis on reasoning-heavy and coordination-heavy families, with and without candidate execution operations.
- **Decision rights [absent]:** No canonical owner or interface adjudicator.
- **Uncertainty handling [direct]:** Candidate execution and bridge operations remain hypotheses; unsettled boundaries are preserved.
- **Verification [direct]:** Direct family comparison and independent synthesis/review.
- **Provenance [direct]:** Link candidates to FE-012A/FE-012B pressure observations and disagreement records.
- **Implementation cost [absent]:** Not measured.
- **Limitations [direct]:** Internal-run evidence, no independently frozen execution vocabulary, and a competing one-grammar explanation.
- **Fidelity check [inference]:** The test records whether separation was actually possible and does not treat candidate operations as accepted.
- **Null-capable future experiment [direct]:** Coordination artifacts may be represented by a modestly refined single grammar, weakening the split hypothesis.

## Statement basis

- **Direct:** Hypothesis, rationale, predictions, weaknesses, and weakening test.
- **Inference:** Input, baseline, fidelity check, and some provenance handling.
- **Proposal-only:** Two-representation operation and output.
- **Absent:** Interface contract, decision authority, and cost estimate.
