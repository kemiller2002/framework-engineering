# A06 — Verification and reassessment triggers

- **Input [direct/inference]:** A provisional judgment, its evidence and confidence, verification needs, and environmental change signals.
- **Operation [direct]:** Verify before closure; in dynamic contexts specify when prior judgments should be reopened; revise or abandon beliefs when kill conditions occur.
- **Output [inference]:** A verified or still-open judgment with recorded reassessment conditions.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Dynamic contexts and revisable evidence-based judgments.
- **Failure conditions [direct/inference]:** No verification before commitment, no trigger in a dynamic context, or a fired kill condition does not reopen the affected judgment.
- **Predicted effect [absent]:** Canon does not state a directional measured effect for prospective triggers against a named control.
- **Measurement [absent]:** No trigger-specific metric is canonical.
- **Named baseline [absent]:** No baseline is specified.
- **Boundary condition [direct]:** The trigger heuristic is provisional and may not be universal.
- **Implementation test [inference]:** Seed a stated kill condition or context change and observe whether the linked judgment is reopened and verification rerun.
- **Decision rights [direct/inference]:** The research constitution requires kill conditions to be respected; ownership of trigger definition and reopening is not assigned.
- **Uncertainty handling [direct]:** Evidence changes belief confidence; unsupported certainty is prohibited.
- **Verification [direct]:** Explicit verification precedes closure.
- **Provenance [inference]:** Link trigger or kill condition to belief, evidence, verification, and resulting decision.
- **Implementation cost [absent]:** Monitoring and retest cost are not estimated.
- **Limitations [direct]:** Evidence comes from provisional synthesis and internal comparisons.
- **Fidelity check [inference]:** Triggers are prospective, name the affected judgment, and can fire without curator discretion.
- **Null-capable future experiment [inference]:** Compare prospective triggers with discretionary review; allow no change or worse false-alarm burden as outcomes.

## Statement basis

- **Direct:** Verification, reassessment, kill-condition, uncertainty, scope, and limitation statements.
- **Inference:** Input/output form, failure path, seeded test, lineage, fidelity check, and future experiment.
- **Proposal-only:** Reassessment-trigger heuristic.
- **Absent:** Directional effect, metric, baseline, trigger thresholds, role assignment, and cost.
