# A07 — Certified inputs and artifact lineage

- **Input [direct]:** Experiment specification, provider/request configuration, raw response, normalized result, source digests, and run metadata.
- **Operation [direct/inference]:** Assign stable artifact and run identifiers; snapshot the request; retain raw and normalized artifacts; record source and request digests; certify dataset completeness before comparison; keep approved instruments frozen unless a blocking defect requires a documented versioned change.
- **Output [direct]:** Canonical run directory, normalized result, normalization certificate, metadata, and downstream comparison inputs.
- **Scope conditions [direct]:** Evidence runs and experiments where reproducibility and dataset completeness matter.
- **Failure conditions [direct]:** Missing, blocked, invalidated, or stale certificate; comparison against uncertified data; unrecorded change to a frozen instrument.
- **Predicted effect [absent]:** No controlled directional outcome is stated for this mechanism.
- **Measurement [direct/inference]:** Certificate state, schema validity, digest agreement, dataset completeness, and presence of required artifacts.
- **Named baseline [absent]:** No matched control is named.
- **Boundary condition [direct]:** The rules govern the research pipeline; a documented blocking defect permits a versioned emergency change.
- **Implementation test [direct]:** Attempt official comparison with a missing or stale certificate; the pipeline must refuse it.
- **Decision rights [direct]:** Approved instrument remains frozen; accepted evidence decision records govern downstream experiment selection.
- **Uncertainty handling [inference]:** Missing or invalid lineage remains a blocking state rather than being silently repaired.
- **Verification [direct]:** Normalize, verify completeness, generate certificates, and compare only certified datasets.
- **Provenance [direct]:** Stable IDs, timestamps, provider/model data, request/result paths, and SHA-256 fields in schemas and run layout.
- **Implementation cost [inference]:** Snapshotting, normalization, certification, hashing, and retained storage.
- **Limitations [direct/inference]:** Repository-wide append history and supersession behavior are not fully defined by the inspected canonical records.
- **Fidelity check [direct]:** Official comparison refuses non-current certificates and consumes only canonical normalized paths.
- **Null-capable future experiment [inference]:** A reconstruction audit can show no improvement in reproducibility or can expose unresolved lineage gaps.
