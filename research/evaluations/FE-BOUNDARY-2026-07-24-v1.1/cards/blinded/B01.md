# B01

- **Input:** Project situation, goals, constraints, reusable method fragments, roles, and work products.
- **Operation:** Select, configure, compose, and adapt method fragments to the situation using an explicit methodology metamodel.
- **Output:** A situation-specific method with process, people, modeling, and work-product definitions.
- **Scope conditions:** Information-intensive development and change work.
- **Failure conditions:** Situation factors are wrong; fragments do not compose; tailoring loses required controls.
- **Predicted effect:** Better situational fit than using a fixed method.
- **Measurement:** Requirement coverage, conformance, effort, and project outcomes.
- **Implementation test:** A separate engineer constructs a method from the same fragments and situation constraints.
- **Decision rights:** Method engineer proposes; project authority accepts tailoring.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Alternative configurations may be retained.
- **Verification:** Metamodel conformance and project review.
- **Provenance:** Fragment-to-method links.
- **Implementation cost:** Repository, modeling, and tailoring expertise.
- **Limitations:** Outcome depends on fragment quality. Direct: original method and metamodel abstracts. Inference: metrics.
