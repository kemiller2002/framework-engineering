# B06

- **Input:** Question, hypotheses, evidence, assumptions, indicators, uncertainties, and new observations.
- **Operation:** Externalize alternatives and evidence, diagnose inconsistency rather than confirming a favorite, record assumptions, define indicators, and reassess when evidence changes.
- **Output:** Comparative evidence matrix, confidence/rationale, indicators, and revised judgment.
- **Scope conditions:** Ambiguous analytic decisions with competing explanations.
- **Failure conditions:** Alternatives are superficial; evidence weights are invented; triggers are not monitored.
- **Predicted effect:** Reduced confirmation bias and earlier correction of invalid judgments.
- **Measurement:** Seeded-bias error, unsupported-conclusion recall, calibration, and correction latency.
- **Implementation test:** Independent analyst applies the matrix and responds to a seeded disconfirming observation.
- **Decision rights:** Analyst assesses; decision owner acts; reviewer challenges assumptions.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Alternatives, confidence, and missing evidence remain explicit.
- **Verification:** Contrarian review and indicator monitoring.
- **Provenance:** Evidence-to-hypothesis links and revision log.
- **Implementation cost:** Added analytic and review time.
- **Limitations:** Coverage is insufficient for Stage B. Direct: method descriptions. Inference: metrics.
