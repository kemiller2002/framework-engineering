# B05

- **Input:** Digital entities, activities, agents, derivations, versions, identifiers, metadata, and source conditions.
- **Operation:** Assign persistent identifiers and rich metadata; represent generation, attribution, derivation, specialization, and revision; retain enough inputs and context for reuse/reconstruction.
- **Output:** Interoperable provenance graph and reusable research objects.
- **Scope conditions:** Digital artifacts exchanged or reused across systems.
- **Failure conditions:** Identifiers are unstable; provenance is incomplete; referenced inputs are inaccessible or mutable.
- **Predicted effect:** Improved findability, impact tracing, interoperability, and reconstruction.
- **Measurement:** Retrieval/reconstruction success, lineage completeness, and affected-descendant recall.
- **Implementation test:** A new operator resolves IDs, traverses lineage, and reconstructs a sampled output from recorded inputs.
- **Decision rights:** Publisher/agent asserts provenance; consumer validates.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Conflicting assertions can coexist with attribution.
- **Verification:** Constraint/digest checks and reconstruction.
- **Provenance:** Core operation.
- **Implementation cost:** Metadata capture, storage, and governance.
- **Limitations:** Does not prescribe domain-specific test design. Direct: specification and primary principles paper. Inference: exact metrics.
