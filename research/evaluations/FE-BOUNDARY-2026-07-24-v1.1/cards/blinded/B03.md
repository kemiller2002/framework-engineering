# B03

- **Input:** Stakeholder needs, requirements, origins, dependencies, design elements, tests, and changes.
- **Operation:** Specify required information items and maintain forward/backward links through origin, refinement, implementation, verification, and revision.
- **Output:** Requirements baseline and trace graph supporting coverage and impact analysis.
- **Scope conditions:** Systems/software products and services across their lifecycle.
- **Failure conditions:** Link rationale is absent; granularity is wrong; links become stale.
- **Predicted effect:** Higher change-impact recall and requirement-verification coverage.
- **Measurement:** Trace precision/recall, orphan rate, and affected-item recall.
- **Implementation test:** Seed a requirement change; independent analyst identifies affected artifacts and tests.
- **Decision rights:** Requirements authority owns baseline; change control approves revisions.
- **Uncertainty handling:** TBDs, rationale, and unresolved conflicts.
- **Verification:** Bidirectional trace and coverage audit.
- **Provenance:** Origin and lifecycle history are core.
- **Implementation cost:** Link creation and maintenance.
- **Limitations:** Traceability alone does not guarantee quality. Direct: standard and original empirical analysis. Inference: metrics.
