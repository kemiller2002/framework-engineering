# B02

- **Input:** System of interest, stakeholders, needs, constraints, lifecycle context, risks, and evidence.
- **Operation:** Select and tailor lifecycle processes; define, analyze, realize, verify, validate, operate, maintain, control, and improve iteratively and recursively.
- **Output:** Controlled lifecycle artifacts, verified system elements, decisions, and improvement actions.
- **Scope conditions:** Human-made systems, components, and systems of systems.
- **Failure conditions:** Stakeholders/needs are incomplete; tailoring omits necessary processes; verification is weak.
- **Predicted effect:** Traceable lifecycle controls improve satisfaction and reduce escaped nonconformance.
- **Measurement:** Verification/validation results, risk, defects, and lifecycle outcomes.
- **Implementation test:** Audit selected processes, inputs, outputs, and tailoring rationale on a project.
- **Decision rights:** Defined organizational/project authorities.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Risk and decision processes.
- **Verification:** Explicit verification and validation processes.
- **Provenance:** Lifecycle information-item configuration.
- **Implementation cost:** Potentially high; tailorable.
- **Limitations:** Does not prescribe one method. Direct: controlling standards. Inference: directional outcome.
