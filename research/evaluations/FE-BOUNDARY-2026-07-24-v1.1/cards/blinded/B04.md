# B04

- **Input:** Activities, events, decisions, participants, messages, data, exceptions, and termination rules.
- **Operation:** Encode control flow with typed activities, gateways, events, sequence/message flows, pools/lanes, loops, and exception behavior; optionally bind to execution semantics.
- **Output:** Inspectable or executable process model.
- **Scope conditions:** Organizational or computational processes with observable flow.
- **Failure conditions:** Semantics are used inconsistently; hidden work is omitted; model and execution diverge.
- **Predicted effect:** Explicit flow models reduce ambiguity and enable conformance checking.
- **Measurement:** Model agreement, conformance violations, execution success, and defect detection.
- **Implementation test:** Parse/validate the model and execute representative paths including exceptions.
- **Decision rights:** Model owner approves; engine enforces executable semantics.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Alternative paths can be modeled; epistemic uncertainty needs extension.
- **Verification:** Schema validation, simulation, and conformance.
- **Provenance:** Optional annotations/version control.
- **Implementation cost:** Modeling expertise and tooling.
- **Limitations:** Native focus is process coordination, not epistemic reasoning. Direct: controlling specification. Inference: comparative metrics.
