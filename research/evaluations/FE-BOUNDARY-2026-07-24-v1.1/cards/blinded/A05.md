# A05

- **Input:** Claims, observations, interpretations, alternatives, uncertainties, and decisions.
- **Operation:** Type each statement; link interpretations to observations; retain competing interpretations; prohibit unsupported promotion between types.
- **Output:** An auditable claim graph with alternatives and uncertainty.
- **Scope conditions:** Evidence-based analysis under ambiguity.
- **Failure conditions:** Types are inconsistently applied; source support is unavailable; alternatives are deleted.
- **Predicted effect:** Relative to untyped notes of equal length, auditors will detect more unsupported conclusions and reconstruct more decision rationales.
- **Measurement:** Seeded unsupported-claim recall and rationale reconstruction accuracy.
- **Implementation test:** Independent auditor follows links without curator assistance and identifies support status.
- **Decision rights:** Analyst types; reviewer challenges; decision owner accepts residual uncertainty.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Explicit values and alternatives.
- **Verification:** Link completeness and contradiction audit.
- **Provenance:** Statement-level source and transformation links.
- **Implementation cost:** Fine-grained annotation.
- **Limitations:** Could be generic evidence structuring. Direct: separation and preservation rules. Inference: prediction.
