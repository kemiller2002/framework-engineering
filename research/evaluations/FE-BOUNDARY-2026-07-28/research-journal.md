## 2026-07-28 — confirming and falsifying passes

### Distinct-discipline pass

Confirming evidence:

- coherent candidate object;
- explicit lifecycle;
- internal corpus, hypotheses, tools, and profile;
- potential human/machine portability problem.

Falsifying evidence:

- each current mechanism maps to existing adjacent work;
- no independent utility evidence;
- no external professional or institutional base;
- repository authority retains low confidence.

Result: distinct-discipline interpretation not supported.

### Integrated-profile pass

Confirming evidence:

- no single comparator foregrounds the exact cross-domain analytical
  framework and record integration;
- the profile can coherently compose established lifecycle, method,
  evidence, semantic, decision, quality, learning, and agent controls;
- current tools execute a pilot subset.

Falsifying evidence:

- integration can be relabeling;
- utility and cost are untested;
- schema enforcement is incomplete.

Result: best normative model, moderate confidence only.

### Repository-program pass

Confirming evidence:

- all direct FE outcome evidence and infrastructure are internal;
- external practice, transfer, curriculum, standards, and community are
  absent.

Falsifying evidence:

- foundations are broadly transferable;
- provider executions and profile structure are not inherently repository
  specific.

Result: accurate current maturity description, not necessarily the endpoint.

### Full-subsumption pass

Confirming evidence:

- collective mechanism mapping is near-complete.

Falsifying evidence:

- systems engineering, method engineering, and design science each leave a
  meaningful unit-of-analysis or integration residual.

Result: individual full subsumption not established; collective functional
subsumption substantially supported.
