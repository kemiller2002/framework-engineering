# A01

- **Input:** A named knowledge artifact plus its stated purpose, components, user group, and uses.
- **Operation:** Record separately (a) what makes the artifact recognizably itself and (b) what operations users can perform with it; flag statements that mix the two.
- **Output:** Two linked inventories with ambiguous items and supporting locations.
- **Scope conditions:** Characterization or redesign of knowledge artifacts with both recognizable structure and intended use.
- **Failure conditions:** Sources do not state purpose or structure; curators classify by intuition; inventories are treated as exhaustive.
- **Predicted effect:** Compared with an equally detailed undivided inventory, reviewers will make fewer unsupported redesign changes while identifying at least as many capability gaps.
- **Measurement:** Unsupported-change proportion and capability-gap recall against an expert key.
- **Implementation test:** Two curators independently apply the rules; each item must include a source location and pass the identity/capability decision question.
- **Decision rights:** Curator proposes; independent reviewer resolves ambiguous items.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Preserve mixed/unknown labels.
- **Verification:** Inter-curator agreement and evidence-location audit.
- **Provenance:** Item-to-source links.
- **Implementation cost:** Two passes plus reconciliation.
- **Limitations:** No matched outcome evidence. Direct: separation and linked evidence are prescribed. Inference: directional prediction and metrics.
