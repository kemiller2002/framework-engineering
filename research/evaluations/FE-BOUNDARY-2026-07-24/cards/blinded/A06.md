# A06

- **Input:** A decision or artifact plus assumptions, tests, observed results, and change indicators.
- **Operation:** Define verification criteria and prospective triggers; when a trigger fires, reopen named assumptions and rerun affected tests.
- **Output:** Verification record, trigger log, and reassessed decision/version.
- **Scope conditions:** Revisable decisions whose environment or evidence may change.
- **Failure conditions:** Triggers are vague; monitoring is absent; reassessment does not reach affected claims.
- **Predicted effect:** Prospective triggers reduce time-to-correction and stale-decision duration compared with discretionary review.
- **Measurement:** Detection latency, stale duration, false-trigger rate, and correction quality.
- **Implementation test:** Inject a boundary-condition change and observe whether the named review path executes.
- **Decision rights:** Owner sets thresholds; monitor fires; reviewer approves revision.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Trigger confidence and unresolved effects remain recorded.
- **Verification:** Seeded-change drills.
- **Provenance:** Trigger-to-assumption-to-test lineage.
- **Implementation cost:** Monitoring and retesting.
- **Limitations:** Generic feedback control is a strong comparator. Direct: verification/reassessment. Inference: matched prediction.
