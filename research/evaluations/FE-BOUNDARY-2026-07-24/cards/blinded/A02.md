# A02

- **Input:** A framework-like artifact, use cases, evidence, constraints, and observed failures.
- **Operation:** Extract structure and behavior, compare them to required uses, propose the smallest redesign addressing evidenced gaps, and retest.
- **Output:** Characterization, gap map, versioned redesign, and test results.
- **Scope conditions:** Explicit knowledge artifacts that can be inspected and revised.
- **Failure conditions:** Requirements are absent; changes lack evidence; new artifact is not retested.
- **Predicted effect:** Redesigns will improve requirement coverage without increasing unsupported content relative to direct revision.
- **Measurement:** Requirement coverage, unsupported-claim rate, and task completion.
- **Implementation test:** A new team receives only the procedure and inputs and produces all four outputs with trace links.
- **Decision rights:** Owner accepts changes; evaluator applies test criteria.
- **Uncertainty handling:** Gaps may remain unresolved.
- **Verification:** Pre/post test against frozen use cases.
- **Provenance:** Each change links to gap, evidence, and test.
- **Implementation cost:** Multi-stage analysis and retest.
- **Limitations:** “Smallest” lacks a formal optimization rule. Direct: workflow stages. Inference: matched prediction.
