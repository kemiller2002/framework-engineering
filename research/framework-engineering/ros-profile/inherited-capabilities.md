# Inherited ROS capabilities

FE inherits the ROS 1.0.0 artifact contract: plain-text canonical records; `RP/JR/EV/HY/TH/EX/DF/CN/GL/MS` identity conventions; lifecycle values; categorical confidence; completion tracked separately; references and reciprocal supersession; filename checks; accepted-content policy; research journals and handoffs; deterministic validation; and generated registries.

FE records retain `id`, `title`, `status`, `version`, `created`, `updated`, `confidence`, `completion`, and `profile`. The FE validator checks the presence of that inherited envelope but does not reimplement ROS lifecycle, identifier, or supersession logic. Generic enforcement stays in ROS.
